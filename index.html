02 / GAME

Split-Time

Unity · C#SoloLuxodd arcade cabinets

A 2D pixel-art puzzle platformer about borrowed time. Press Split and a past version of you, an echo, replays your moves. Then you solve each room together with yourself: one holds the door, the other runs through.

46
levels across 5 worlds
4
bosses, each with its own mechanic
2
layouts: landscape and portrait cabinet
  • Echo mechanic: record a run, split, and play alongside your own replay.
  • Bosses: intro title cards, story dialogue, defeat sequences and a clock you recover from each one.
  • Full game loop: level captions, animated results, game-over cards, achievements, a skin wardrobe, leaderboard and an ending comic.
  • Arcade-ready: cloud save, hints, tutorials, auto-advance and two cabinet screen layouts.

03 / GAME

Cross the Road

Unity · C#Solo3D · WebGL · arcade

An endless 3D road-crossing game with blocky characters. Hop over busy roads and rivers, ride the logs and see how far you get before the traffic gets you.

  • Endless roads: lanes, cars and logs generated ahead of the player and the camera.
  • Fair leaderboard: competitive mode uses a fixed seed, so everyone gets the same road.
  • Arcade pressure: three lives, revive at your last safe spot, and a timer that starts an auto-scrolling camera.
  • Platform integration: paid continues, online leaderboard and session tracking through the Luxodd SDK.

04 / GAME

Pause to Play

Unity · C#Solo2D · arcade

A comedy platformer that breaks the rules on purpose. To start the game you press Pause. Buttons become platforms, the volume slider is an elevator, and menu words like "Inverting" and "Camera Zoom" change how the level works. Every room asks you to do the thing a game normally wouldn't let you do.

05 / GAME

Nodulus

Unity · C#Arcade port3D puzzle

A calm 3D puzzle game: slide the blue piece along the grid to cover every node and finish on the green check. I didn't create Nodulus. I reworked an existing version for Luxodd's arcade cabinets.

  • Arcade rework: a timer and score HUD so each puzzle becomes a run you can score.
  • Cabinet-ready: joystick and button controls, the Luxodd boot screen and platform integration.
  • Level select: named puzzles with your best score shown under each one.
